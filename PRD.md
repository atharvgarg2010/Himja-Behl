# PRD.md

# HIMJA BEHL — Wedding Styling Platform

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name
HIMJA BEHL

### Product Type
Premium personal-brand website and lead-generation platform for a wedding wardrobe stylist.

### Primary Purpose
The website exists to:
1. Establish Himja Behl as a premium wedding wardrobe stylist.
2. Showcase her styling philosophy, work, and client journeys.
3. Build trust through strong visual presentation and social proof.
4. Help prospective clients understand the styling process.
5. Convert interested visitors into qualified styling enquiries.
6. Provide visitors with a personalized initial styling direction based on their preferences.

The website should feel closer to a **luxury fashion editorial / digital magazine** than a conventional service-business website.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

### Brand
HIMJA BEHL is a premium wedding styling brand focused on curating clothing and complete wardrobe direction for:
- Brides
- Grooms
- Close family members
- Couples
- Families preparing for multi-function weddings

Himja's role is not wedding décor planning.
Her expertise is centered around **what people wear, how the wardrobe comes together, and how each individual look fits into the larger wedding story.**

---

## 3. Product Goals

### Primary Goals

#### G1 — Build Premium Perception
The website should immediately communicate:
- Luxury
- Taste
- Curation
- Fashion expertise
- Personal attention
- Editorial sophistication

A visitor should feel that Himja provides a highly curated service rather than generic outfit recommendations.

#### G2 — Generate Qualified Leads
The primary conversion goal is to get potential clients to submit a styling enquiry.
The website should collect enough information to understand:
- Who needs styling
- Wedding requirements
- Number of functions
- Location
- Wedding date
- Preferred aesthetic
- Colour preferences
- General expectations

#### G3 — Establish Trust
Visitors should understand:
- Who Himja is
- How she approaches styling
- What working with her feels like
- What previous clients experienced
- What results her styling can create

#### G4 — Differentiate the Brand
The website should avoid looking like a generic:
- Wedding planner website
- Fashion blog
- Bridal boutique
- Portfolio template
- AI website
- Generic freelancer website

The central identity should be:
> **A personal, premium, editorial wedding wardrobe styling experience.**

---

## 4. Target Audience

### Primary Audience

#### Brides
Women preparing for weddings who want help creating a cohesive wardrobe across multiple functions.
Typical needs:
- Outfit selection
- Styling direction
- Colour coordination
- Function-wise wardrobe planning
- Accessories
- Overall visual consistency

#### Grooms
Men looking for a curated wardrobe for:
- Wedding
- Reception
- Sangeet
- Mehendi
- Engagement
- Other wedding functions

#### Families
Parents, siblings, and close family members who want their wardrobes to coordinate with the overall wedding aesthetic.

#### Couples
Couples who want their individual wardrobes to complement each other without looking overly matched.

---

## 5. User Problems

Potential clients may experience:
- Too many outfit options
- Difficulty deciding what to wear for each function
- Lack of a cohesive wardrobe
- Difficulty coordinating colours
- Uncertainty about trends vs timeless choices
- Difficulty finding the right designers or pieces
- Fear of looking repetitive across functions
- Difficulty balancing personal taste with wedding aesthetics
- Stress caused by making many individual styling decisions

The product should position Himja as the person who **reduces this complexity through curation.**

---

## 6. Core Product Experience

The website should communicate a simple journey:

> **Discover → Understand → Trust → Enquire → Receive a Personal Direction → Connect with Himja**

The visitor should never feel like they are navigating a complicated platform.
The experience should be highly visual and editorial.

## 15. Styling Process

### Purpose
Explain what happens after a client decides to work with Himja.
The process should make the service feel:
- Structured
- Personal
- Professional
- Premium
- Easy to understand

### Suggested four-stage process:

#### 01 — DISCOVER
Understand the client.
This includes:
- Personal style
- Wedding requirements
- Functions
- Preferences
- Expectations

#### 02 — CURATE
Build the wardrobe direction.
This involves:
- Outfit direction
- Designers / pieces
- Colours
- Silhouettes
- Function-wise styling

#### 03 — REFINE
Bring everything together.
This stage focuses on:
- Coordination
- Accessories
- Details
- Balance
- Final styling decisions

#### 04 — DELIVER
Create a clear, cohesive wardrobe ready for the wedding.
The exact wording can evolve with Himja's actual service process.

## 19. Style Archetypes

The initial product can use a limited set of style directions.
Possible archetypes:

### Modern Romantic
Soft, elegant, contemporary and expressive.

### Refined Traditionalist
Classic Indian styling with carefully curated traditional elements.

### Contemporary Minimal
Clean, understated and intentional.

### Regal Classic
Rich, statement-led and ceremonial.

### Modern Maximalist
Bold, expressive and fashion-forward.

### Effortless Fusion
A balance between traditional and contemporary styling.

These are **styling directions**, not personality types.
The product must not make psychological claims based on the answers.

AI should remain invisible as a technology feature.

## 37. Product Principles

### Principle 1 — The Stylist Comes First
Technology should enhance Himja's expertise, not replace it.

### Principle 2 — Editorial Over Template
Every major section should feel intentionally designed.

### Principle 3 — Curation Over Quantity
Show fewer, stronger pieces rather than overwhelming the visitor.

### Principle 4 — Story Over Sales Pitch
Client journeys should demonstrate value through stories rather than aggressive selling.

### Principle 5 — Premium Means Intentional
Luxury should come from:
- Typography
- Photography
- Space
- Composition
- Details
- Consistency

Not simply from gold colours or decorative elements.

### Principle 6 — Conversion Without Pressure
The visitor should always know how to enquire, but the website should never feel pushy.

### Principle 7 — Human Expertise Remains Central
Personalization can make the first interaction more engaging, but the ultimate value comes from Himja's human styling expertise.

---

## 38. Final Product Definition

HIMJA BEHL is a premium digital front door for a wedding wardrobe stylist.

It is not merely a portfolio.
It is not a generic wedding website.
It is not an AI styling application.

It is an editorial brand experience designed to:
Make visitors understand Himja's taste, trust her expertise, imagine their own wedding wardrobe through her perspective, and take the first step toward working with her.
