# CONTENT.md

## Purpose

This document contains the approved content direction, placeholder copy, sample editorial content, form copy, CTA language, and other written content required to build the HIMJA BEHL website.

This is a content source-of-truth for the development agent.

The content should feel:

- Premium
- Editorial
- Personal
- Sophisticated
- Warm
- Fashion-conscious
- Intelligent
- Confident
- Never overly salesy
- Never generic wedding-industry copy

Avoid cliché wedding language such as:

- "Your dream wedding"
- "Making your dreams come true"
- "Where dreams meet reality"
- "Perfect wedding"
- "Magical moments"
- "Turning dreams into reality"
- Excessive use of words like "royal", "fairytale", "magical", etc.

The writing should feel closer to a luxury fashion publication or a refined personal styling studio than a conventional wedding vendor.

## Story Copy

A wedding is never just one outfit.

There is the ceremony, the evening, the smaller gathering before it, the photographs, the people standing beside you and the atmosphere that ties everything together.

Himja's approach to styling begins with looking at the celebration as a whole.

She works closely with her clients to understand who they are, what they want to feel like, what the occasion demands and how every look can exist within the larger visual story.

From the bride and groom to their immediate family, every wardrobe is considered with intention — silhouettes, colours, fabrics, jewellery, accessories and the way everything will come together in the room.

The process is personal.

Sometimes that means discovering a designer you had never considered. Sometimes it means refining an outfit already in your wardrobe. # 19 — CONTENT RULES

The website should never sound like a generic wedding planner.

Himja is a stylist and wardrobe curator.

The content should consistently communicate:

- Personal styling
- Wardrobe curation
- Individuality
- Cohesion
- Colour
- Silhouette
- Fabric
- Accessories
- Occasion
- Venue awareness
- Lighting awareness
- Photography awareness
- Shopping assistance
- Family styling
- Couple styling

Do not describe Himja as:

- Wedding planner
- Wedding decorator
- Event planner
- Décor stylist
- Event designer

unless the actual business offering changes.

# 20 — PLACEHOLDER CONTENT RULE

The testimonials and client names in this document are fictional placeholders for development and design purposes only.

They must NOT be presented as genuine client testimonials on the production website.

Before launch, replace:

- Client names
- Testimonials
- Journey details
- Client photographs
- Designer references
- Event details
- Dates
- Any claims about actual work

with approved real client content.

The agent should clearly mark placeholder content in code/data where appropriate so it is easy to replace later.

---

# 21 — TONE OF VOICE

Write like a luxury fashion editor who understands people.

Not like:

- A wedding marketplace
- A wedding planner
- A corporate agency
- An AI startup
- A generic luxury brand generator

The voice should be:

Quietly confident.

Specific rather than exaggerated.

Personal rather than promotional.

Elegant rather than complicated.

Premium without constantly saying "premium".

The writing should leave room for photography, typography and whitespace to communicate as much as the words.

---

# 22 — FINAL CONTENT PRINCIPLE

HIMJA BEHL DOES NOT SIMPLY HELP PEOPLE FIND OUTFITS.

SHE CURATES HOW PEOPLE BELONG TO THE CELEBRATION.

Every piece of website copy should reinforce this idea without repeatedly stating it word-for-word.
