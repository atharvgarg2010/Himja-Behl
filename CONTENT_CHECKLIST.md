# Himja Behl Website — Content Collection Checklist

Welcome to the final content collection phase! The website structure, design, and animations are fully complete. Currently, the site uses placeholder "dummy" text and images to demonstrate the layout. 

Please use this checklist to provide the final text and high-resolution images so we can swap them in and launch!

---

## 1. Hero Section (First thing users see)
**Role**: Sets the immediate premium tone of the brand.
* **Current Dummy Headline**: "Curating The Edit. One Celebration At A Time."
  * **Need**: Your preferred main headline.
* **Current Hero Image**: `hero-illustration.jpg` (A cutout of a couple/bride).
  * **Need**: 1 High-resolution, striking image (preferably a cutout style or a very clean editorial shot) that represents the peak of your styling work.

## 2. The Story (About Himja)
**Role**: Establishes your philosophy, authority, and personal connection with the client.
* **Current Dummy Headline**: "Style isn't about choosing more. it's about choosing right."
  * **Need**: A strong philosophy headline.
* **Current Dummy Quote**: *"I don't believe in dressing people for a wedding. I believe in understanding the celebration first — and then finding the clothes that belong in it."*
  * **Need**: A personal quote from you summarizing your approach.
* **Current Dummy Body Text**: 4 paragraphs explaining how you look at the celebration as a whole, rather than just dressing people for an occasion.
  * **Need**: Your actual "About Me / My Philosophy" text. (Can be 3-4 paragraphs).
* **Current Dummy Image**: `himja-portrait.jpg`
  * **Need**: 1 High-resolution, professional portrait of you (Himja) looking editorial and approachable.

## 3. The Marquee (Scrolling Text Banner)
**Role**: A dynamic, scrolling banner that breaks up the page with brand catchphrases.
* **Current Dummy Words**: "CURATED NOT COMPROMISED", "EVERY LOOK HAS A STORY", "THE ART OF THE WEDDING WARDROBE"
  * **Need**: 3 to 4 short, punchy statements or brand values to scroll continuously.

## 4. Client Journeys (Portfolio / Case Studies)
**Role**: This is the most important section. It showcases your past work as high-end editorial fashion stories. We currently have 3 dummy journeys set up.
For **EACH** Journey (Please provide 3 to 6 journeys total), we need:
* **Journey Name**: (e.g., "The Heritage Edit")
* **Client Names**: (e.g., "M & S" or "Rahul")
* **Event Context**: (e.g., "Royal Jaipur Palace Wedding")
* **Cover Image**: 1 High-res portrait image for the homepage card.
* **Full Journey Page Content**:
  * **Introduction**: 1-2 sentences setting the scene.
  * **The Brief**: What did the client ask for?
  * **The Process**: How did you source/curate it?
  * **The Result**: The final look description.
  * **Client Testimonial**: A quote from the client.
  * **Gallery Images**: 3 to 6 high-res images from this specific wedding/styling journey to create the editorial spread.

## 5. The Process
**Role**: Explains how you work with a client from start to finish.
* **Current Dummy Steps**: 
  1. **DISCOVER**: Initial consultation and vision boarding.
  2. **CURATE**: Sourcing, designer appointments, and selection.
  3. **REFINE**: Fittings, tailoring, and styling the final details.
  4. **DELIVER**: The final wardrobe handover.
  * **Need**: If your actual process differs, please provide the 4 steps, including a Title and a 1-2 sentence description for each.

## 6. Footer & Contact Details
**Role**: Where clients find your official information and links.
* **Need**: 
  * Official Contact Email
  * Contact Phone Number (or WhatsApp Business number)
  * Links to Social Media (Instagram, Pinterest, etc.)
  * Studio Address (Optional, if you take in-person consultations)

### Image Guidelines for Submission
- Please provide images via a **Google Drive** or **Dropbox** link to preserve high quality (do not send via WhatsApp).
- Ensure images are high resolution (at least 2000px wide for full-screen shots).
- If you have specific preferences on which image goes where, please rename the files (e.g., `Hero-Main.jpg`, `Journey1-Cover.jpg`, `Himja-Portrait.jpg`).
